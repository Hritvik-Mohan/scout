# Software Engineering Job Lead Sources

No directory can be literally exhaustive—company career pages and small
communities change constantly—but this is a comprehensive source universe for
Bengaluru and India-compatible remote SDE roles.

## Highest-priority sources for your search

These should produce most of the useful leads:

1. [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) — Bengaluru roles, recruiter
   posts, referrals.
2. [Naukri](https://www.naukri.com/) — essential for recruiter discovery in
   India.
3. [Instahyre](https://www.instahyre.com/) — curated technology hiring; postings
   are manually reviewed.
   [Instahyre](https://help.instahyre.com/en/article/how-to-post-a-job-on-instahyre-14il3zs/)
4. [Cutshort](https://cutshort.io/jobs) — Indian product companies, startups and
   remote engineering.
5. [Hirist](https://www.hirist.tech/) — technology-specific Indian board with
   strong Bengaluru coverage. [Hirist](https://www.hirist.tech/)
6. [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs) — startup and scale-up roles with
   direct hiring-team access.
7. [YC Work at a Startup](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs/) — YC startups,
   including Bengaluru and remote roles.
   [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs/)
8. Direct company career pages and ATS feeds — usually the freshest and most
   authoritative source.
9. [HiringCafe](https://hiringcafe.com/jobs) — newer search platform indexing
   openings directly from employer career pages.
   [HiringCafe](https://hiringcafe.com/jobs)
10. [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/) — newer job index, matching, tracking and
    application tooling; it says it indexes company pages and more than 50
    boards. [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/)
11. [Levels.fyi Jobs](https://www.levels.fyi/jobs) — engineering jobs with level
    and compensation context; it currently includes Bengaluru roles.
    [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/jobs)
12. [Hacker News Who’s Hiring](https://news.ycombinator.com/submitted?id=whoishiring)
    and [HN Jobs](https://news.ycombinator.com/jobs) — direct startup and
    founder-posted openings.
13. [Remotive](https://remotive.com/), [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com/) and
    [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/) — global remote discovery.
14. VC portfolio job boards such as
    [a16z Portfolio Jobs](https://portfoliojobs.a16z.com/jobs) and Techstars.
15. Employee referrals, former colleagues, alumni and engineering communities.

## Direct company ATS sources

This is the most important category for automation because it discovers jobs at
the original employer source.

### Best public-feed candidates

- Greenhouse
- Lever
- Ashby
- SmartRecruiters

Greenhouse exposes public job-board GET endpoints without authentication; Lever
exposes published postings; Ashby returns current postings, remote status,
workplace type and optional compensation; SmartRecruiters exposes active company
postings. [Greenhouse](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html),
[Lever](https://github.com/lever/postings-api),
[Ashby](https://developers.ashbyhq.com/docs/public-job-posting-api),
[SmartRecruiters](https://developers.smartrecruiters.com/docs/endpoints)

## Recommended Scout rollout

I would not activate every source simultaneously—the duplication and low-quality
volume would overwhelm the pipeline.

- **Automate next:** Ashby, SmartRecruiters, HiringCafe-compatible discovery, YC
  jobs, Hacker News hiring threads, Jobgether and selected company ATS boards.
- **Use alerts/manual imports:** LinkedIn, Naukri, Instahyre, Cutshort, Hirist,
  Wellfound and Simplify.
- **Check weekly:** remote boards, VC portfolio boards and specialist
  communities.
- **Prioritize direct applications:** always replace an aggregator URL with the
  employer’s original careers-page URL when possible.
